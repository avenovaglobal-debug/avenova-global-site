SERVICES / DELIVERY BOUNDARIES

Clear scope.
Better delivery.

A professional engagement begins by agreeing on the problem, deliverables, dependencies, acceptance checks, and what is outside the scope. The options below describe possible engagements, not a claim that every capability is already operating at scale.

▣

01 / DIGITAL PRESENCE

Website launch

A focused, responsive website to explain a service, organisation, or offer and give visitors a clear next step.

Possible deliverables

  • One landing page or an agreed small set of pages
  • Mobile and desktop layouts
  • Navigation and contact pathway
  • Basic browser and interaction checks
  • One consolidated revision round, if agreed
  • Handover notes for delivered files
⌘

02 / WORKFLOW IMPROVEMENT

Workflow review

Map a repetitive business process and identify realistic improvements before committing to implementation.

Possible deliverables

  • Current-process summary
  • Problem and friction-point list
  • Options ranked by effort and value
  • Dependency and risk notes
  • Suggested implementation phases
SCOPED FIRST
▤

03 / SOFTWARE PLANNING

Product planning

Turn an early software idea into a clearer product definition and a sensible, testable build sequence.

Possible deliverables

  • Target-user and problem statement
  • Core user journeys
  • Prioritised feature list
  • Technical and operational assumptions
  • Risks, unknowns, and staged roadmap

Delivery standard: Work is accepted subject to project fit, available capacity, agreed requirements, and the safeguards appropriate to the task. Complex or security-sensitive work requires separate assessment.

EXCLUSIONS UNLESS AGREED SEPARATELY

Keep the engagement
realistic and transparent.

The following are not included in the initial service offer or price unless explicitly assessed, priced, and agreed in writing.

01

Third-party costs

Domain registration, paid hosting, premium themes or assets, paid software, paid APIs, and other subscription or usage fees.

02

Advanced functionality

E-commerce, payment processing, user accounts, custom backend systems, and complex integrations.

03

Additional services and guarantees

Copywriting, branding, photography, specialist work beyond agreed deliverables, unlimited revisions, ongoing maintenance, SEO guarantees, or guaranteed business results.

Pricing is quoted after requirements are understood. Any indicative ranges discussed during planning are proposals to validate, not guaranteed market rates or fixed public prices.
